Hermes Agent helps its owner understand email, organize inboxes, prepare replies, and plan around authorized calendars.

It connects personal, tutoring, and work accounts separately. Its purpose is to support the owner's own daily tasks.

Read the privacy policy ↗

Access with a purpose.

Google permissions are granted through Google's consent screen. Each connected account has its own authorization and access settings.

01

Email

Gmail & iCloud Mail

Read requested messages, organize mail, and prepare drafts. Sending a message requires the owner's approval of the recipient and final text.

02

Personal & tutoring calendars

Google Calendar

Read schedules and manage authorized events. Keep lessons and personal commitments in their own calendars.

03

Work availability

Availability only

Check busy periods for planning. The work-calendar connection does not request permission to read meeting details or edit meetings.

The owner stays in control.

The assistant runs in the owner's self-hosted Hermes environment. When AI processing is requested, selected content may be sent to the model providers configured in that environment.

Authorization tokens and conversation records are handled by the assistant environment. This informational website has no account connection form and does not display mailbox or calendar data.

Permission is separate from approval.

Google's Gmail permission technically includes sending. The account adapter exposes reading, organization, and drafting; the assistant's operating rules require explicit approval before a message is sent. The iCloud connection is configured for IMAP access.
