Private use and access

Hermes Agent is a private assistant integration operated by Maksym Babak for his own authorized accounts. These pages explain that installation; they do not offer a public assistant service, account registration, or access to the operator's inboxes and calendars.

Authorized tasks

The integration supports reading and organizing authorized mail, preparing drafts, managing authorized personal and tutoring calendar events, and checking work-calendar availability. Sending email requires the owner's approval of the specific recipient and final message. Access to work accounts remains subject to the account administrator's rules.

Owner control

The owner should review assistant output and proposed changes before relying on them. Account authorizations can be revoked through the relevant provider. The privacy policy describes data handling, processing providers, retained records, and deletion requests.
